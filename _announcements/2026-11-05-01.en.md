We are pleased to announce the addition of the **[ODB (Operon DataBase)](https://operondb.jp/)** **[RDF dataset](https://rdfportal.org/dataset/odb/)** to RDF Portal.

ODB brings together information on known operons across multiple genomes, drawing on published studies of operon organization and transcriptional units. It provides a resource for studying gene organization and comparing operons across organisms.

The RDF dataset links known operons to their organisms, the proteins encoded by their constituent genes, and supporting publications, using NCBI Taxonomy, NCBI Protein, and PubMed identifiers. Its availability in RDF Portal enables SPARQL queries to retrieve operons for a given organism, identify proteins associated with the same operon, and explore supporting literature. These links also facilitate integration with other life science RDF datasets.

For more information, please visit the **[ODB website](https://operondb.jp/)** and refer to the publications describing the [2011 update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21051344/) and the [original database](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16381886/).

We are pleased to announce the release of **JoGo RDF** in RDF Portal.

**The Joint Open Genome and Omics Platform (JoGo)** constructs and catalogs human haplotypes primarily using third-generation long-read sequencing technologies.

JoGo 1.0 comprises 258 datasets from five population groups and provides a haplotype catalog covering 19,194 protein-coding genes based on MANE annotations. The data use the ATCG haplotype nomenclature newly developed by JoGo, which assigns consistent identifiers to haplotypes at four levels: amino acid sequence (A), transcript (T), coding sequence (C), and whole gene (G). JoGo also catalogs single-nucleotide variants, insertions, and deletions, with annotations based on comparisons with telomere-to-telomere (T2T) genome assemblies of non-human primates.

The RDF representation enables SPARQL queries and integration with other life science datasets in RDF Portal.

For more information, please visit the [JoGo website](https://jogo.csml.org/) and refer to the [JoGo publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1232).

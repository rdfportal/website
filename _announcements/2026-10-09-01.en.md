We are pleased to announce the release of RDFized **LOTUS** in RDF Portal. **LOTUS** is a database of natural product occurrences that links chemical compounds to the organisms in which they have been reported and the supporting literature.

**LOTUS** integrates chemical structures, biological taxonomy, and literature references to support research on natural products and their distribution across organisms. It also provides chemical classifications from NPClassifier and ClassyFire, along with links to external resources such as PubChem, NCBI Taxonomy, and Wikidata.

RDFized **LOTUS** is an RDF representation of the **LOTUS** v11 CSV release, published on April 13, 2026. It represents each reported occurrence as a link between a chemical structure, an organism, and a supporting reference. This enables SPARQL queries to explore these relationships and supports integration with other life science datasets in RDF Portal.

For more information, please visit the  [LOTUS website](https://lotus.naturalproducts.net/) and refer to the [LOTUS publication](https://elifesciences.org/articles/70780).
